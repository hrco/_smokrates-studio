NoPanicPost

NoPanicPost is a personal tool. It sends finished videos from the creator's own computer to the creator's TikTok inbox as drafts.

It does not publish anything. The creator always taps Post in TikTok.

How it works

  1. Sign in with TikTok.
  2. Send a video from your computer.
  3. Review the draft in TikTok and tap Post.

What it uses

  • It uses only the video.upload scope.
  • The tokens stay on the creator's computer.
  • There is no server-side storage.
  • There is no analytics.
