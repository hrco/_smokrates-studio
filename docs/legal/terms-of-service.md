# NoPanicPost Terms of Service

Last updated: 14 October 2026

These terms apply when you use NoPanicPost, a personal command-line tool that uploads your own videos to your TikTok account as drafts.

## Use

- Use NoPanicPost only with a TikTok account that you own or are allowed to manage.
- Upload only content that you have the rights to, including music, images and video.
- Follow the TikTok Terms of Service and Community Guidelines. Label AI-generated content in TikTok when you post it.

## What NoPanicPost does

NoPanicPost sends a video to your TikTok inbox as a draft. It does not publish anything. You review the draft in TikTok, add the caption and settings, and decide whether to post it.
