# TikTok For You ranking and what moves a video to wide distribution

Research date: 2026-10-13. Scope: organic consumer TikTok (global), with US and rewards-region differences called out. Ads are mentioned only where they change how to read organic metrics.

How to read the citations: a page is **primary** when the sentence was taken from TikTok’s own rendered page or a search-index extract of a TikTok URL that several independent pages quote the same way. Creator Academy articles often returned only site navigation when opened directly on 2026-10-13; facts from those URLs are marked **academy snippet** and should be treated as TikTok’s creator-education copy, not as a published weight table. Third-party numbers are labeled **measurement**, **advice**, or **folklore**. Folklore means a figure is widely repeated and was not found in a TikTok document.

TikTok has never published the model weights, a test-batch size, or a view threshold that promotes a video. The only explicit strong-vs-weak comparison still on an official page is from 18 June 2020: finishing a longer video outweighs a weak signal such as viewer and creator sharing a country. Current pages add that, for most people, interactions (including time spent watching) outweigh content metadata and device settings, and that the system ranks by predicted actions (finish, skip, like, share, comment, favorite, follow, profile interaction, soundtrack tap, Not Interested, time spent). Eligibility rules — not the ranker — are what quietly remove unoriginal, low-quality, spam, and some policy-edge videos from For You while leaving them up for search and profile.

## What has TikTok officially said about For You ranking in 2024, 2025, and 2026?

### Takeaway
The public ranking description has been stable since 2020 and was restated, not replaced, in 2025–2026: personalized prediction of interactions, with watch behavior weighted above passive metadata, follower count not a direct factor, and a separate eligibility layer that can keep a video off For You without deleting it. TikTok still does not publish relative weights among like, comment, share, save, replay, or follow.

### Cited Findings
- **18 June 2020, still live (historical, and still the clearest weight sentence).** Newsroom “How TikTok recommends videos #ForYou” says recommendations combine user interactions (videos liked or shared, accounts followed, comments posted, content created), video information (captions, sounds, hashtags), and device and account settings (language, country, device type). Device and account settings “receive lower weight … since users don't actively express these as preferences.” “A strong indicator of interest, such as whether a user finishes watching a longer video from beginning to end, would receive greater weight than a weak indicator, such as whether the video's viewer and creator are both in the same country.” “Neither follower count nor whether the account has had previous high-performing videos are direct factors,” though a larger follower base can still produce more views indirectly. New users may pick interest categories; otherwise they see popular videos. “Your first set of likes, comments, and replays” starts early recommendations. Long-press “Not Interested,” hiding a creator or a sound, and reporting a video “contribute to future recommendations.” The feed generally will not show two videos in a row with the same sound or by the same creator. TikTok says it does not recommend duplicated content, content already seen, or spam. Some videos that are not yet broadly liked are shown on purpose, both to diversify the feed and to learn what a wider audience likes. Videos just uploaded or still under review, and videos “seeking to artificially increase traffic,” “may be ineligible for recommendation.” Graphic medical procedures and legal but regulated goods may also be ineligible for a general audience. Weights are reassessed over time; the post does not give coefficients. — [TikTok Newsroom, 18 June 2020](https://newsroom.tiktok.com/en-us/how-tiktok-recommends-videos-for-you)
- **Transparency page, live 13 Oct 2026, copyright line © 2025, no revision date on the page.** For You is a prediction score: “an estimate of how likely you are to interact with” each video; highest scores are served. Pipeline: (1) retrieve videos that passed moderation and are eligible, (2) predict interactions, (3) rank by those scores, (4) similarity check that replaces near-duplicates (example given: same sound), (5) rules, including regional creators in the feed and ineligibility of content from accounts under 16. Collaborative filtering is described: if another person engaged with two videos you engaged with, the system may predict you will engage with a third they engaged with. Not Interested “will let us know you are not interested in this type of content and we will limit how much of that content we recommend.” The appendix is explicitly “non-exhaustive” and “dynamic.” Personalization inputs named: region, device language, operating system, videos recently watched, finished, liked, or skipped, and follow relationships. Predicted actions named: like, share, comment, or mark Not Interested; follow the author or interact with their profile; finish, skip, or favorite; spend a certain amount of time viewing; tap the soundtrack. Video-side signals named: time posted, region posted, author’s language setting, soundtrack, length, hashtags. The page points to the Help Center as the up-to-date source. It does not rank those predictions against each other. — [Introduction to the TikTok recommendation system](https://www.tiktok.com/transparency/en/recommendation-system)
- **Help Center “How TikTok recommends content,” linked from that transparency page.** The English URL did not render in a direct fetch on 13 Oct 2026. Search-index extracts, repeated across later writeups, say: interest picks at signup seed For You, Explore, and LIVE; if skipped, recent posts popular with others are shown, filtered by location and language. Three factor families — user interactions, content information, user information — predict relevance. “The importance of these factors can shift over time” and “may also change within different areas of the app.” For You examples: interactions = like, share, comment, watch in full or skip, and followers you follow back; content information = sounds, hashtags, number of views, country published; user information = device settings, language, location, time zone and day, device type. “For most users, user interactions, which may include the time spent watching a video, are generally weighted more heavily than others.” Some recommender systems also use “interactions from others who appear to have similar interests.” Turning personalized feeds off (documented as a setting; region list not in the extract) makes Following and Friends chronological and turns For You into a Popular feed of regional and international popular content. — [Help Center article linked by TikTok](https://support.tiktok.com/en/using-tiktok/exploring-videos/how-tiktok-recommends-content); same factor language is summarized by [viral.app, updated 30 Sep 2026](https://viral.app/blog/guides/tiktok-algorithm-explained)
- **Creator Academy “Understanding the Recommendation System.”** Search index: “Last updated: Sep 25, 2026,” “Views: 789K.” Direct open on 13 Oct 2026 returned navigation only, so this is an academy snippet. It describes the same pipeline in creator language: review videos recently watched, finished, liked, skipped, commented on, or saved; filter by taste plus location and the region where the video was posted; estimate the chance of liking, sharing, commenting, or skipping; sort by that score; replace similar items for variety; apply rules such as including creators in the viewer’s region. Swiping past a type of video (example: ASMR) reduces how much of that type is shown. No coefficients. — [Creator Academy recommendation intro](https://www.tiktok.com/creator-academy/article/guidelines-recommendation-system-intro)
- **20 Dec 2022 newsroom, historical, still the sourced origin of “Why this video.”** Share panel → “Why this video.” Reasons TikTok said it would show: user interactions (content watched, liked, or shared; comments; searches); accounts followed or suggested accounts; content posted recently in the viewer’s region; popular content in the viewer’s region. — [Newsroom, 20 Dec 2022](https://newsroom.tiktok.com/learn-why-a-video-is-recommended-for-you)
- **Support FAQ “Why this post,” search-index extract, undated in the extract but live in 2026 results.** Example reasons now also include: you commented on, liked, shared, or watched similar posts; popular in your country; created recently; “This video is longer and you seem to enjoy longer videos”; you’re following this creator. After a For You refresh, TikTok says it shows popular content so new interactions can reshape the feed. — [Support FAQ](https://www.tiktok.com/support/faq_detail?id=7581820706028296760)
- **For You eligibility standards, two dated versions.** Version released 17 Apr 2024, effective 17 May 2024: the system “takes into account factors including likes, shares, comments, searches, diversity of content, and popular videos.” Low views “may be due to a lack of community engagement rather than being ineligible.” Some themes (dieting, extreme fitness, sexual suggestiveness, sadness, overgeneralized mental-health information) could stay eligible but were interrupted if repetitive. Accounts under 16 were ineligible. — [FYF standards, 2024 H1 version](https://www.tiktok.com/community-guidelines/en/fyf-standards?cgversion=2024H1update). Current version released 25 Aug 2026, effective 24 Sep 2026: the system “looks at things like what you like, share, comment on, and search for, as well as what’s trending.” A video that misses For You “may still” be found by search or on the creator’s account. Low views do not by themselves mean a rule was broken. Analytics can show whether a video was made ineligible for recommendation. Repetitive extreme fitness, dieting, sexual suggestiveness, sadness, or overgeneralized mental-health advice is interrupted; during crisis or unrest, repetitive recommendations may also be interrupted. — [FYF standards, effective 24 Sep 2026](https://www.tiktok.com/community-guidelines/en/fyf-standards)
- **Creator Academy analytics article, search index dated 10 Feb 2026 (academy snippet).** “While watch time is more heavily weighted,” likes, comments, and shares still matter; retention rate is what the article tells creators to watch first, engagement second. — [Using analytics to improve video performance](https://www.tiktok.com/creator-academy/article/analytics-tool-video-performance)
- **What TikTok has not said.** No 2024, 2025, or 2026 primary page found that assigns a percentage to completion, replays, likes, comments, shares, saves, follows, or profile visits, or that says shares outrank comments outrank likes. Pages that print “60%+ completion triggers a push” or “shares > comments > likes” are not quoting a TikTok document. — contrast [viral.app, 30 Sep 2026](https://viral.app/blog/guides/tiktok-algorithm-explained), which makes this point, with unsourced rankings such as [IDeqo](https://ideqo.com/resources/tiktok-algorithm-guide/) and [Everything-PR, updated 26 Sep 2026](https://everything-pr.com/tiktok-algorithm-ranking-signals)

### Inferences
- The 2020 “finishes a longer video” sentence is still the only official strong-vs-weak comparison. Later documents are compatible with it: time spent and finishing are inside the interaction family that “generally” outweighs metadata, and length itself is only a video feature plus a user-facing “you seem to enjoy longer videos” reason. Length is not a stated bonus.
- Likes, comments, shares, follows, favorites/saves, profile interactions, soundtrack taps, skips, and Not Interested are all **targets the model predicts**, which means each can move the score. Official text does not say which positive action is largest. Treating “shares are 3x likes” as fact overclaims the documents.
- “Number of video views” as a content feature (Help Center) means popularity of the video already in distribution can influence who sees it next. That is different from follower count, which TikTok says is not a direct factor. A video can snowball on its own early performance without the account’s past hits being a direct input.
- Search is an official **viewer** signal for For You (“what you … search for,” 2026 standards; searches were already a “Why this video” reason in 2022). That is not the same claim as “keywords inside the video raise its For You score.”

### Gaps
- The English Help Center page did not render on 13 Oct 2026. The three-family weighting sentence is from search extracts, not a full-page capture. A report should re-open that URL before quoting it as verbatim.
- No primary page dates a 2025 or 2026 change to the relative weight of watch time versus likes. Articles that announce a “watch-time algorithm update” in 2026 do not cite a TikTok changelog.
- Caption text is named in the 2020 newsroom and is absent from the 2026 transparency appendix and from the Help Center For You bullet captured here. Both current lists say they are examples or non-exhaustive, so disappearance from the short list is not evidence that captions were dropped.

## How does the cold-start / test-batch system work for a new video and a new account?

### Takeaway
TikTok describes exploration, moderation gating, and per-video scoring, not a published ladder of 200–500 then 1,000–10,000 views. Batch sizes, “50 engagement points,” and “the first hour decides it” are folklore. The closest measured timeline is a vendor count of how often videos are still at zero views, which is not a test of ranking thresholds.

### Cited Findings
- **New viewer, not new creator (official, 2020, and restated in the Help Center extract).** A new account is invited to pick interests (pets, travel, and similar). That seeds For You and, in the Help Center wording, Explore and LIVE. If the person skips, TikTok shows recent popular posts appropriate for a broad audience, influenced by location and language, and suggests popular creators. The first likes, comments, and replays “initiate an early round of recommendations.” — [Newsroom, 18 June 2020](https://newsroom.tiktok.com/en-us/how-tiktok-recommends-videos-for-you); [Help Center](https://support.tiktok.com/en/using-tiktok/exploring-videos/how-tiktok-recommends-content)
- **New video gate (official).** A video enters the recommendation pool only after moderation. Content that violates guidelines is removed; content “not suitable for a broad audience” is made ineligible. Only videos that pass are eligible to be scored. — [Transparency recommendation page](https://www.tiktok.com/transparency/en/recommendation-system). Separately, the 2020 post says videos “just been uploaded or are under review” may be ineligible until that review finishes. That is the only official comment on a delay before distribution, and it gives no minute count. — [Newsroom, 18 June 2020](https://newsroom.tiktok.com/en-us/how-tiktok-recommends-videos-for-you)
- **Exploration is official; a fixed test batch is not.** TikTok says it sometimes recommends a video that “doesn’t appear to be relevant to your expressed interests or [has not] amassed a huge number of likes,” so the system can learn what is popular with a wider audience and so viewers discover new categories. “Why this video” can say the post was created recently or is popular in the country. — [Newsroom, 18 June 2020](https://newsroom.tiktok.com/en-us/how-tiktok-recommends-videos-for-you); [Newsroom, 20 Dec 2022](https://newsroom.tiktok.com/learn-why-a-video-is-recommended-for-you)
- **Account history is not a direct input (official, 2020, still the standing statement).** Past high-performing videos are not a direct factor. A new creator is therefore not officially handicapped by having no hit history, and is also not officially given a numeric “new account boost.” A large account can still get more views because followers exist. — [Newsroom, 18 June 2020](https://newsroom.tiktok.com/en-us/how-tiktok-recommends-videos-for-you)
- **Low views are not a verdict (official, 2026).** “If a video isn’t getting many views, it also doesn’t necessarily mean it broke a rule.” Analytics is where ineligibility is supposed to show. — [FYF standards, effective 24 Sep 2026](https://www.tiktok.com/community-guidelines/en/fyf-standards)
- **Vendor measurement of zero-view timing, not a threshold test.** viral.app says it tracked more than 1 million public videos posted March–August 2026 and read final views on 1 Oct 2026. Among about 24,000 seen in the first 30 minutes, 14.6% showed 0 views; 57% of those later got any views, 38% later reached 100+, 10% later reached 1,000+. Among about 74,000 seen at 30 minutes to 2 hours, 6.8% were still at 0; 41% later got views, median 14, and 1.5% later reached 1,000+. At 2–6 hours, 5.6% of about 190,000 were at 0; 0.9% of those later reached 1,000+. At 18–30 hours, 4.8% of about 550,000 were at 0; 15% ever got a view and 0.3% reached 1,000+. Accounts under 1,000 followers were far more often still at 0 (11.4% at 30 minutes–2 hours) than accounts over 1 million (under 0.1%). The search index’s “published” date on that URL is 14 Nov 2025, which cannot be the study date; use the in-article window. This is a vendor panel of tracked accounts, not a random sample of all uploads, and it does not observe TikTok’s internal batch IDs. — [viral.app, “0 views after an hour”](https://viral.app/blog/guides/tiktok-0-views-after-an-hour)
- **Folklore, unsourced, do not treat as TikTok policy.** Repeated blog figures include an initial audience of 200–300 people in 1–2 hours and a “50 engagement point” cutoff ([Napolify, 12 June 2025](https://napolify.com/blogs/news/tiktok-300-view-jail)); 100–500 users in the first 1–3 hours, with 10–20% of them existing followers and a 60%+ completion ideal ([Hashmeta](https://hashmeta.com/insights/how-tiktok-algorithm-works)); new accounts (0–30 days) getting 500–1,000 test views instead of 300 (same Napolify piece); a 0–10 / 10–35 / 35–60 minute decision inside the first hour ([Eye of Riyadh, 27 Apr 2026](https://www.eyeofriyadh.com/news/details/why-the-first-hour-matters-for-tiktok-views)). None of these cite a TikTok document, a dataset, or a methodology. They contradict each other on the batch size.
- **Washington Post, 10 Dec 2025, is about the viewer graph, not creator cold start.** The Post built its own map of 121,000 videos from who saw them. It reports that TikTok tracks watch time, likes, swipes, and comments, and that a 2022 ByteDance paper resembles industry embedding recommenders. TikTok’s comment to the Post was that the company is transparent and that its site lists signals. The Post does not estimate batch sizes or weights. — [Washington Post, 10 Dec 2025](https://www.washingtonpost.com/technology/interactive/2025/tiktok-algorithm-video-map-interests/)

### Inferences
- A workable description that stays inside the documents: moderate → small exploratory distribution (including to people who have not followed, because follower count is not required) → score predicted interactions, especially time watched and finishing → expand, hold, or stop. The “small” size is unpublished. Recency is a real, user-visible reason a new post is shown, so cold start is partly “this was just posted,” not only “this account is trusted.”
- For a brand-new account, the official story is the same per-video test plus no follower seed. There is no sourced “probation” or “new-creator bonus” number. The viral.app split by follower size is consistent with the 2020 indirect-follower effect (bigger accounts are rarely stuck at zero) without proving a different formula.
- “Dead after 60 minutes” is too strong. In that vendor panel, a large minority of videos still at zero before the two-hour mark later received views, but almost none that were still at zero a day later reached 1,000. The data supports “a day at zero is usually final,” not “the first hour is the only window.”

### Gaps
- No credible public source shows internal batch sizes, score cutoffs, or how many followers versus interest-matched strangers are in the first slice. Anyone stating 300, 500, or 50 points is repeating folklore.
- TikTok has not said how long “under review” lasts, or what share of uploads are held out of For You for review rather than for weak engagement.
- The viral.app zero-view study is the best timeline found and is still unaudited. Its sampling frame is accounts inside that product.

## Which signals are leading indicators of a push versus lagging or weak?

### Takeaway
Leading, in TikTok’s own words: predicted watch time and finishing a longer video, inside a family of interactions that also includes skip, like, share, comment, favorite, follow, and profile interaction. Weak or indirect: device and country match, hashtags and sounds as topic matchers rather than quality scores, follower count, and past viral videos. Posting-time “best hour” charts are not a ranking factor TikTok has published.

### Cited Findings
- **Strong, named by TikTok (2020, still live).** Finishing a longer video from start to finish outweighs same-country match. — [Newsroom, 18 June 2020](https://newsroom.tiktok.com/en-us/how-tiktok-recommends-videos-for-you)
- **Generally heavier (Help Center extract).** For most users, interactions, “which may include the time spent watching a video,” outweigh content information and user information. — [Help Center](https://support.tiktok.com/en/using-tiktok/exploring-videos/how-tiktok-recommends-content)
- **Creator-facing restatement (academy snippet, index date 10 Feb 2026).** Retention first; likes, comments, and shares second; “watch time is more heavily weighted.” — [Creator Academy analytics article](https://www.tiktok.com/creator-academy/article/analytics-tool-video-performance)
- **The prediction list is the real inventory of leading signals (transparency appendix, live 13 Oct 2026).** Like, share, comment, Not Interested, follow the author, interact with the profile, finish, skip, favorite, time spent, soundtrack tap. Rewatches are not a separate bullet. Extra watch time from a replay is inside “spend a certain amount of time,” and the 2020 post names replays only as an early taste signal on a **new viewer’s** account (“likes, comments, and replays”). — [Transparency recommendation page](https://www.tiktok.com/transparency/en/recommendation-system); [Newsroom, 18 June 2020](https://newsroom.tiktok.com/en-us/how-tiktok-recommends-videos-for-you)
- **Favorites / saves.** “Favorite a video” is an official prediction target. Academy recommendation copy, Sep 2026 snippet, also says the system reviews what you “saved.” No weight versus comments or shares is given. — [Transparency recommendation page](https://www.tiktok.com/transparency/en/recommendation-system); [Creator Academy recommendation intro](https://www.tiktok.com/creator-academy/article/guidelines-recommendation-system-intro)
- **Follows and profile visits.** “Follow the video’s author or interact with their profile” is a prediction target, so a follow or a profile tap from the video is an official positive signal for that viewer-video pair. Follows also change the follower’s future For You. Neither is quantified. — [Transparency recommendation page](https://www.tiktok.com/transparency/en/recommendation-system)
- **Hashtags, sounds, captions: matching, not the push.** 2020: captions, sounds, hashtags are video information. 2026 appendix: soundtrack, length, hashtags, post time, region, author language. Help Center For You bullet: sounds, hashtags, view count, country. These tell the system what the video is and who might be interested. They are not described as strong indicators of interest. A same-country match is the explicit weak example. Using a sound can also create a **sound-page** traffic source and can cause the similarity check to avoid stacking the same sound. — [Newsroom, 18 June 2020](https://newsroom.tiktok.com/en-us/how-tiktok-recommends-videos-for-you); [Transparency recommendation page](https://www.tiktok.com/transparency/en/recommendation-system)
- **Follower count and past hits: not direct (2020, never retracted in the pages read).** Indirect path only: more followers can mean more people already likely to see the post. View count of the **current** video is a content feature, so traction on this upload can matter even when account history does not. — [Newsroom, 18 June 2020](https://newsroom.tiktok.com/en-us/how-tiktok-recommends-videos-for-you); [Help Center](https://support.tiktok.com/en/using-tiktok/exploring-videos/how-tiktok-recommends-content)
- **Posting time.** “Time zone and day” are user-information signals about the viewer, in the lower-weighted family. That is not “Tuesday 9 a.m. ranks higher.” Creator Academy suggests posting on followers’ active days and at most two hours before the peak “to build momentum.” That is distribution advice, not a disclosed weight. Buffer’s “Sunday 9 a.m.” slot, cited in a 2026 roundup of 7.1 million posts, is a third-party timing study, not TikTok. — [Help Center](https://support.tiktok.com/en/using-tiktok/exploring-videos/how-tiktok-recommends-content); [Creator Academy analytics article](https://www.tiktok.com/creator-academy/article/analytics-tool-video-performance); Buffer figure as relayed by [TTS Vibes, 3 Oct 2026](https://insights.ttsvibes.com/tiktok-first-hour-engagement-impact-on-reach/)
- **#fyp / #foryou.** No primary page found that says those tags increase For You eligibility. viral.app says Creator Academy has said getting on For You is not about the #fyp tag; that sentence was not in the academy extracts captured here, so it stays a secondary claim. — [viral.app, 30 Sep 2026](https://viral.app/blog/guides/tiktok-algorithm-explained)
- **Rewards formula is a different ranking.** Since the Creator Rewards Program launch writeup of 18 Mar 2024, payout uses originality, play duration (watch time and finish rate), search value, and audience engagement, plus ad-watch value of the audience. Support text in 2026 still computes RPM from average watch time and finish rate, search traffic, location, engagement, and “your viewer’s ad watch time.” That formula pays creators. It is not the For You ranker. — [Newsroom, 18 Mar 2024](https://newsroom.tiktok.com/introducing-the-new-creator-rewards-program); [Support, how rewards work](https://support.tiktok.com/en/business-and-creator/creator-rewards-program/how-rewards-work)

### Inferences
- If a creator can only watch one family of numbers, official text points at watch time and completion (retention), then other interactions. Likes alone are a real but unnamed-as-weaker member of that family. A video with high likes and very short watches is not what TikTok describes as a strong interest signal.
- Replays are best read as extra watch time plus, for brand-new viewers, an early taste signal. They are not documented as a separate multiplier above completion.
- Hashtags and sounds help the retriever find an interest cluster and can add a sound-page path. They do not, on the documents, cause a weak video to be pushed. Generic tags are not a substitute for watch behavior.
- Follower count is a distribution head start, not a score bonus. A new account can be recommended; it just has fewer people who will see the video because they follow the account.

### Gaps
- No official ordering of share vs comment vs save vs follow vs replay. Practitioner rankings that use 98/100-style scores are editorial.
- Unfollow is not named in any primary page read for this note. Do not treat “unfollows kill the video” as documented. It may sit inside follow-graph features; TikTok has not said so.
- Whether average percentage viewed or raw seconds is the feature is not specified. Both “finish” and “time spent” are predicted, so a long video can win on seconds without a high completion rate, which is exactly what the 2020 sentence implies. The crossover point is unpublished.

## What negative signals suppress a video?

### Takeaway
Two different mechanisms get called “suppression.” The ranker predicts skip and Not Interested and down-ranks for people likely to do those things. Separately, eligibility rules remove whole classes of video from For You (unoriginal, spam, manipulation, some harm categories) even if the people who do see them engage. Short watches under a stated number of seconds are a Rewards rule (5 seconds), not a published For You cutoff.

### Cited Findings
- **Skip.** Predicted alongside finish. Swiping past a type of video reduces more of that type for that viewer (academy ASMR example). The 2020 post’s “content you’d prefer to skip” is the general framing. No official “under 2 seconds” or “under 3 seconds” threshold for the ranker. — [Transparency recommendation page](https://www.tiktok.com/transparency/en/recommendation-system); [Creator Academy recommendation intro](https://www.tiktok.com/creator-academy/article/guidelines-recommendation-system-intro)
- **Not Interested.** Three official roles. (1) Viewer control: long-press limits how much of “this type” is recommended. (2) Ranking: the model predicts the probability the viewer will mark Not Interested. (3) Money: a view from someone who marked not interested is excluded from Creator Rewards qualified views. Creators do not get a public “Not Interested count” in the analytics descriptions found here. — [Transparency recommendation page](https://www.tiktok.com/transparency/en/recommendation-system); [Newsroom, 18 June 2020](https://newsroom.tiktok.com/en-us/how-tiktok-recommends-videos-for-you); [Support, how rewards work](https://support.tiktok.com/en/business-and-creator/creator-rewards-program/how-rewards-work)
- **Report.** Long-press Report sends the video to moderation. It is an enforcement input, not described as a graded engagement feature like skip. — [Transparency recommendation page](https://www.tiktok.com/transparency/en/recommendation-system)
- **Hide creator or sound.** 2020: choosing to hide a creator or a sound contributes to future recommendations. That suppresses that creator or sound for that viewer, not necessarily the video globally. — [Newsroom, 18 June 2020](https://newsroom.tiktok.com/en-us/how-tiktok-recommends-videos-for-you)
- **Very short watches as a payout exclusion, not a For You rule.** Qualified Rewards views must be longer than 5 seconds (Support and Creator Academy). The EEA terms also say qualified views are those a user views for five seconds or more and that are not marked not interested. Search views need at least 30 seconds to count toward Rewards, per the academy FAQ snippet. None of these sentences say For You stops distributing at 5 seconds. — [Support, how rewards work](https://support.tiktok.com/en/business-and-creator/creator-rewards-program/how-rewards-work); [Creator Academy, Creator Rewards](https://www.tiktok.com/creator-academy/article/creator-rewards-program); [EEA Creator Rewards terms](https://www.tiktok.com/legal/page/global/tiktok-creator-rewards-program-eea/en)
- **Unoriginal and reused (eligibility, not a down-rank).** Integrity and Authenticity, released 25 Aug 2026, effective 24 Sep 2026: “Content is also ineligible for the FYF if it includes unoriginal or reused material without anything new.” IP violations can be removed entirely; unoriginal-without-anything-new is specified as For You ineligibility. The rendered page’s “More information” accordion did not expand in this fetch, so example lines were not captured directly. Several readers of the expanded text say the ineligible examples, carried over from the 13 Sep 2025 guidelines, are “reused or unoriginal content posted without creative edits, such as clips that show someone else’s watermark or logo” and “low-quality or minimally edited content, such as short clips made from GIFs only.” Treat those two examples as secondary quotations of TikTok’s expanded guidelines, not as lines extracted here. — [Integrity and Authenticity, effective 24 Sep 2026](https://www.tiktok.com/community-guidelines/en/integrity-authenticity); example lines as read by [ShapelessAI, 7 Oct 2026](https://shapelessai.com/notes/unoriginal-content-rules-youtube-tiktok-instagram) and [Crown Media, updated 7 Oct 2026](https://crownmediacreative.com/tiktok-says-content-low-quality/)
- **Creator Academy originality definition (academy snippet; official account video 11 Mar 2025 says the same four buckets).** Unoriginal includes content copied completely; largely repurposed without creative edits; combined from multiple sources with little added information or value; someone else’s visible watermark or superimposed logo. It “may be removed from the For You feed.” It is also ineligible for Creator Rewards, which uses “originality” as a key metric. ShapelessAI, citing an academy page it dates as last updated 23 Dec 2025, says the insufficient-edit examples include subtitles taken from the original audio, basic text summaries over someone else’s clip, and “only splicing clips together.” Those examples were not re-rendered here. — [Creator Academy originality policy](https://www.tiktok.com/creator-academy/article/tiktok-originality-policy); [TikTok @creatoracademy, 11 Mar 2025](https://www.tiktok.com/@creatoracademy/video/7480639551011114256); example gloss in [ShapelessAI](https://shapelessai.com/notes/unoriginal-content-rules-youtube-tiktok-instagram)
- **Spam, fake engagement, manipulation (official, 24 Sep 2026).** TikTok does not allow accounts that manipulate the platform or the trade of artificial engagement. Deceptive accounts can be banned or restricted, including loss of posting, top search, or For You. “Content that tries to manipulate people into giving gifts or inflating likes or follows isn’t eligible for the FYF.” Fake likes and followers are removed. Automation and bypass tools can mean removal or bans. The 2020 post already said duplicated content, already-seen content, spam, and artificial-traffic videos may be ineligible. — [Integrity and Authenticity, effective 24 Sep 2026](https://www.tiktok.com/community-guidelines/en/integrity-authenticity); [Newsroom, 18 June 2020](https://newsroom.tiktok.com/en-us/how-tiktok-recommends-videos-for-you)
- **Other FYF ineligibility that is not an engagement score.** Misinformation that may cause moderate harm, and unverified crisis or civic content or content under fact-check review, is For You-ineligible (24 Sep 2026). Suspected paid political advertising is ineligible. Under-16 creators are ineligible. Repetitive sensitive themes are interrupted rather than always banned. — [Integrity and Authenticity](https://www.tiktok.com/community-guidelines/en/integrity-authenticity); [FYF standards, effective 24 Sep 2026](https://www.tiktok.com/community-guidelines/en/fyf-standards); [Transparency recommendation page](https://www.tiktok.com/transparency/en/recommendation-system)
- **Shop unoriginal rules are stricter and are not the consumer For You spec.** TikTok Shop Seller University (page dated 23 Dec 2025 in one extract) prohibits unoriginal livestream and video commerce, including stitch without new commentary, blurred watermarks, and “sandwich frames.” A 7 Oct 2026 Shop page describes a Content Authorization Tool for unoriginal/pirated-content violations. Do not generalize Shop enforcement to organic For You. — [Seller University, unoriginal content](https://seller-ph.tiktok.com/university/essay?knowledge_id=7654197957084929&default_language=en); [Content Authorization Tool, 7 Oct 2026](https://seller-us.tiktok.com/university/essay?knowledge_id=744803252258574&lang=en)
- **“Distribution capped at followers only” and “four classifier signals” (audio fingerprint, template spam, and so on) are not in the guidelines text captured here.** A 2026 SEO-style FAQ states that as if it were an internal disclosure. It is unsourced. The documented outcome is For You ineligibility, which can look like a collapse to followers and profile visitors because those paths still work. — contrast [FYF standards](https://www.tiktok.com/community-guidelines/en/fyf-standards) with [Social Boost Digital](https://socialboostdigital.com/faq/tiktok-unoriginal-content-flag-2026)

### Inferences
- A video can be suppressed with no strike and no takedown. The 2026 standards tell creators to look at analytics for an ineligibility flag rather than infer a shadowban from a low view count. Low views remain ambiguous: weak retention and ineligibility look the same until that flag is checked.
- Watermarked reposts and GIF-only clips are the examples secondary readers keep quoting. For AI video, the risk TikTok actually names is “reused material without anything new” and “largely repurposed … without creative edits,” not the use of AI as such. Minimal edits (speed, stickers, subtitles of the original audio) are described as not enough in academy-adjacent readings, which matters for slideshow and clip-compilation formats.
- “Not Interested” hurts in two layers: it trains the viewer’s future feed, and it is a negative prediction target on the video. It is not documented as a global kill switch after a fixed count of taps.
- The 5-second Rewards rule is easy to misuse as an algorithm rule. A 4-second watch can still be a For You view. It just does not pay.

### Gaps
- No official count of skips, Not Interested taps, or reports that flips a video from eligible to suppressed.
- Whether reuploading your own video counts as “reused” is not answered on the rendered integrity page. NoobClaw (4 Sep 2026, updated discussion of the 24 Sep 2026 text) says the guidelines do not forbid reposting your own video and do not define a duplicate penalty; that is a reading of an absence, not a safe-harbor from TikTok. — [NoobClaw](https://noobclaw.com/blog/post-same-video-twice-tiktok-2026/)
- Community Guidelines strikes, 90-day expiry, and “account standing” ladders are described by secondary sites and were not opened in the enforcement chapter for this note.

## How do Search, Following, Friends, and LIVE differ from For You, and does TikTok SEO affect distribution in 2026?

### Takeaway
For You is the personalized interest ranker and the main discovery surface. Following and Friends are relationship feeds (and can be chronological when personalization is off). LIVE is a separate recommender seeded by the same broad signal families. Search is both its own traffic source and a viewer signal that feeds For You. TikTok confirms that search queries and search traffic matter for discovery and for Rewards RPM. It does not confirm that spoken keywords, on-screen text, or captions are For You ranking features, and the “3x if you hit all three text layers” claim is unsourced.

### Cited Findings
- **For You vs other feeds (Help Center extract).** Each area of the app can weight factors differently. For You is the personalized stream. With personalized feeds off, For You becomes a Popular feed, and Following and Friends “may feature content from creators you follow in chronological order.” LIVE and STEM (select regions) are in the same non-personalized list. — [Help Center](https://support.tiktok.com/en/using-tiktok/exploring-videos/how-tiktok-recommends-content)
- **Friends.** Japanese Help Center “友達タブ,” captured in search: the Friends tab shows content from mutual followers, accounts you follow, and, in some regions, people you may know (contacts or Facebook friends if the user syncs them). Some users see a different tab instead of Friends. This is a social graph, not an interest test. — [Support, Friends tab (JA)](https://support.tiktok.com/ja/using-tiktok/exploring-videos/friends-tab)
- **LIVE.** Interest selection at signup helps form the LIVE feed as well as For You. Shop’s LIVE playbook (US, 18 May 2026) is a commerce dashboard (impressions, enter-room rate, watch duration, product conversion), not a description of organic LIVE ranking for ordinary creators. No consumer page found that lists LIVE’s prediction targets in the detail given for For You. — [Help Center](https://support.tiktok.com/en/using-tiktok/exploring-videos/how-tiktok-recommends-content); [TikTok Shop LIVE traffic playbook, 18 May 2026](https://seller-us.tiktok.com/university/essay?knowledge_id=3175988075644686&lang=en)
- **Search still works when For You does not.** 2026 FYF standards: a video kept off For You may still be found through search or the creator’s profile. — [FYF standards, effective 24 Sep 2026](https://www.tiktok.com/community-guidelines/en/fyf-standards)
- **Search is a For You input on the viewer side.** 2026 standards list “what you … search for” among things the recommendation system looks at. The 2022 “Why this video” reasons include searches. — [FYF standards](https://www.tiktok.com/community-guidelines/en/fyf-standards); [Newsroom, 20 Dec 2022](https://newsroom.tiktok.com/learn-why-a-video-is-recommended-for-you)
- **Search is a measured traffic source and a payout input.** Creator Academy analytics snippet: traffic sources include For You, profile, and Search; creators should read the queries that led to a post and “include these in future titles.” Rewards RPM includes “search value (the amount of search traffic that your content generates, including video views on the search page).” Academy FAQ: search views count toward Rewards only if watched at least 30 seconds, and only from program countries. — [Creator Academy analytics article](https://www.tiktok.com/creator-academy/article/analytics-tool-video-performance); [Support, how rewards work](https://support.tiktok.com/en/business-and-creator/creator-rewards-program/how-rewards-work); [Creator Academy Rewards](https://www.tiktok.com/creator-academy/article/creator-rewards-program)
- **Creator Search Insights is a real product, not a ranking disclosure.** Creator Academy’s nav (live 13 Oct 2026) includes Creator Search Insights, a detail page, a “content gap” tab, and articles titled “Driving video traffic: Search” and “Getting discovered with Search.” Those article bodies did not render in this pass. The existence of the tool shows TikTok wants creators to target queries. It does not publish how text is extracted. — [Creator Academy index](https://www.tiktok.com/creator-academy/en/article/ai-generated-content-label)
- **SEO-blog claims that failed the primary-source test.** Multiple 2026 guides say TikTok ranks search, and sometimes For You, using speech-to-text, OCR of on-screen text, and captions, and one says hitting all three is a “3x discovery tailwind.” None quote a TikTok page for the 3x, for OCR, or for a mid-2025 “auto-transcript became a ranking input” change. The transparency appendix’s video features are time, region, author language, soundtrack, length, and hashtags — not transcript or on-screen text. That list is non-exhaustive, so it does not disprove text features. It also does not confirm them. — [shortgen.io, 22 June 2026](https://shortgen.io/blog/tiktok-seo-2026); [Blitzcut, 29 Jan 2026](https://blitzcutai.com/blog/tiktok-seo-guide-2026); [Voqusa, 15 May 2026](https://www.voqusa.com/en/blog/tiktok-seo-guide-2026); contrast [Transparency appendix](https://www.tiktok.com/transparency/en/recommendation-system)
- **X, 13 Oct 2026 sample.** A Latest search for algorithm / For You / watch time / unoriginal since June 2026, filtered to posts with at least 15 likes, did not return operator teardowns with data. The relevant posts are viewer anecdotes: Not Interested is an imperfect control ([@soliloquirk, 4 Oct 2026](https://x.com/soliloquirk/status/2106832035444904367); [@HanDoesHisBest, 15 Aug 2026](https://x.com/HanDoesHisBest/status/2088457762770411919)); one advice post says to tap Not Interested, then search and like what you want, and not to watch unwanted videos to the end ([@sharpmahn, 1 Aug 2026](https://x.com/sharpmahn/status/2083637252274454572)). A high-engagement AI-Shop thread claims the algorithm “doesn’t know the model isn’t real” and rewards watch time and purchases, and states TikTok Shop GMV of $66 billion in 2026 and a $75,000 commission anecdote ([@ashercrw, 15 July 2026](https://x.com/ashercrw/status/2077328251588841494)). No TikTok document or dataset is linked for the GMV or the commission. Treat as a claim.

### Inferences
- Distribution and discovery are different doors. For You is where wide reach comes from, and it is gated by eligibility plus predicted engagement. Search and profile are the residual doors when For You is shut, and search is also how a video keeps earning Rewards search-value after the initial push. A creator optimizing only for For You can ignore keywords and still be consistent with the ranker documents; a creator optimizing for Rewards or for durable traffic cannot.
- Spoken keywords and on-screen text are plausible search features because TikTok already transcribes for captions and tells creators to put proven queries in titles. Plausible is not measured. Until a primary page or a controlled test shows it, “TikTok SEO” should be described as a search-traffic tactic, not as a For You boost.
- Following traffic is a weak sign of a push. If analytics show mostly Following or profile, the video is being watched by people who already had a path to the account. For You share is the distribution indicator TikTok’s own analytics article points at.

### Gaps
- Article bodies for “Driving video traffic: Search” and “Getting discovered with Search” were not captured. They may contain the missing official line on captions or spoken words.
- No study found that isolates caption keywords, spoken keywords, and on-screen text while holding watch time constant.
- Friends-tab ranking versus pure recency, when personalization is on, is not specified beyond the JA support description of who is eligible to appear.
- EU/EEA non-personalized feed is a known DSA-era product. The Help Center extract documents the toggle and does not name the EU. viral.app (30 Sep 2026) says the toggle is available in the EU and Kazakhstan. That region list was not confirmed on a TikTok page in this pass. — [viral.app](https://viral.app/blog/guides/tiktok-algorithm-explained)

## What changed in 2025–2026: originality, AI labels, length, carousels, Series, Studio, and the US algorithm?

### Takeaway
The documented 2025–2026 changes are eligibility and governance, not a new public ranking formula. Unoriginal content is explicitly For You-ineligible. The creator AI label is officially said not to change distribution if the video otherwise complies, but a viewer control can still turn AI down in that person’s feed. Upload length went to 60 minutes and in-app recording to 10, which does not by itself rank longer videos higher. Creator Rewards, not For You, is what requires videos over one minute and excludes Photo Mode. In the US, the recommendation model is being retrained on US data under TikTok USDS Joint Venture; TikTok has not published a new signal list for that model.

### Cited Findings
- **Originality, 2025 into 2026.** The sentence in force since 24 Sep 2026 is FYF ineligibility for unoriginal or reused material “without anything new.” Secondary readers say the watermark and GIF-only examples were already in the 13 Sep 2025 text and were kept. Creator Academy and the official @creatoracademy video (11 Mar 2025) define the four unoriginal buckets and tie them to For You removal and to Rewards. This is a distribution penalty, not an automatic deletion. — [Integrity and Authenticity, effective 24 Sep 2026](https://www.tiktok.com/community-guidelines/en/integrity-authenticity); [Creator Academy originality policy](https://www.tiktok.com/creator-academy/article/tiktok-originality-policy)
- **AI labels do not, by TikTok’s statement, reduce distribution.** Support, “About AI-generated content”: creators must label realistic AI images, audio, and video. The creator label reads “creator labeled as AI-generated.” “Turning on the AI-generated content setting won't affect the distribution of your video as long as it doesn't violate our Community Guidelines.” Auto label (“AI-generated”) can be applied for TikTok AI effects or C2PA Content Credentials and cannot be removed by the creator. Unlabeled realistic AI “may” be removed. Misleadingly labeling real footage can also be removed. Creator Academy FAQ snippet: “Applying the label will not have an impact on organic engagement with your content, provided that your content isn't violative and meets our For You feed eligibility standards.” Shop education pages say content is not demoted solely because the setting is on, and auto-disclosed content is not penalized solely for the label. Those Shop lines are seller education, consistent with the consumer support sentence, not a separate study. — [Support, About AI-generated content](https://support.tiktok.com/en/using-tiktok/creating-videos/ai-generated-content); [Creator Academy AI label](https://www.tiktok.com/creator-academy/en/article/ai-generated-content-label); [TikTok Shop AIGC notes](https://seller-us.tiktok.com/university/essay?knowledge_id=491489038501663&lang=en)
- **A viewer slider can still cut AI reach without “penalizing” the label.** Newsroom (Sub-Saharan Africa desk), search index 10 Mar 2026: TikTok was testing a Manage Topics control for how much AI-generated content appears in For You; labeling efforts had covered “over 1.3 billion videos”; invisible watermarks were being tested on TikTok tools such as AI Editor Pro and on C2PA uploads. If viewers turn AI down, labeled AI is shown less often to those viewers. That is a demand-side control, not a statement that the label lowers the video’s score. — [Newsroom, SSA, indexed 10 Mar 2026](https://newsroom.tiktok.com/tiktok-ssa-shares-more-ways-to-spot-shape-and-understand-ai-generated-content?lang=en-ZA)
- **Label volume, not reach impact (25 Sep 2025).** Sixth EU Disinformation Code transparency report: in the reporting period, creator-labeled AI videos grew 36% to more than 8.7 million, and automatically labeled AIGC increased 81% to about 5.5 million. Policy-violating AIGC removals fell 53% to fewer than 25,000. These are labeling and enforcement counts, not views per labeled video. — [Newsroom, 25 Sep 2025](https://newsroom.tiktok.com/tiktok-sixth-disinformation-code-transparency-report?lang=en-150)
- **Community Guidelines rewrite, effective 24 Sep 2026.** Same release date as the originality sentence. Realistic AI that shows scenes or people must be labeled; unlabeled content may be removed, restricted, or labeled by TikTok. Deceptive accounts can lose search and For You visibility. This is enforcement, not a new engagement weight. — [Integrity and Authenticity, effective 24 Sep 2026](https://www.tiktok.com/community-guidelines/en/integrity-authenticity)
- **Do not use the “AI videos get 2.1x the views” figure as a ranking result.** Revid.ai (15 Sep 2026) compared 9,940 TikToks tagged AI-generated with 252,107 tagged real in its own index from May 2025 to Sep 2026: average views 1.4 million vs 661,400, and about 19.5% vs 9.5% passing 1 million views. The same writeup says the index skews viral and like rate was lower on the AI set (3.9% vs 7.7%). It is not a random sample of uploads, not a label on/off test, and not TikTok data. — [Revid.ai](https://www.revid.ai/case-studies/ai-videos-vs-real)
- **Length limits changed; optimal length did not get an official number.** Current Camera tools Help Center (Polish locale, captured 13 Oct 2026): videos recorded in TikTok up to 10 minutes; videos uploaded up to 60 minutes. If a sound is selected first, the sound’s length caps the video. A March 2024 archive of the English camera-tools page still said 60 seconds recorded and 3 minutes uploaded, so the ceiling has moved since then. Secondary pages disagree on whether every account sees 60-minute upload. TikTok has not published a best length. “21–34 seconds” and “7–15 seconds for maximum reach” are blog estimates. Median length in one vendor panel barely moved: about 19.5 seconds (Oct 2025–Mar 2026) to 20 seconds (Apr–Aug 2026) on roughly 5,100 accounts, and 16 seconds median across a wider 3.3 million-video set. — [Support, camera tools (PL)](https://support.tiktok.com/pl/using-tiktok/creating-videos/camera-tools); [Internet Archive, English camera tools, 30 Mar 2024](https://web.archive.org/web/20240330030913/https://support.tiktok.com/en/using-tiktok/creating-videos/camera-tools); [viral.app length trends](https://viral.app/blog/insights/short-form-video-trends)
- **Longer videos and Rewards, not a For You bonus.** Transparency lists length as a video feature. “Why this post” can cite that the viewer likes longer videos. Creator Rewards requires at least one minute, at least 1,000 qualified For You views, original high-quality video, not a Duet or Stitch, not Photo Mode, not an ad. Academy RPM article (index date 18 Aug 2026) claims users “now spend 50% of their time watching longer content,” that watch time of 1-minute-plus videos was “5x higher than short videos” in “the last 6 months,” and that viewership of longer videos “quintupled.” The six-month window is not dated in the snippet. That statistic is an explanation for the Rewards program, not a For You weight change. — [Transparency recommendation page](https://www.tiktok.com/transparency/en/recommendation-system); [Creator Academy Rewards](https://www.tiktok.com/creator-academy/article/creator-rewards-program); [Creator Academy RPM factors](https://www.tiktok.com/creator-academy/article/RPM-understanding-the-four-key-factors); [Newsroom, 18 Mar 2024](https://newsroom.tiktok.com/introducing-the-new-creator-rewards-program)
- **Photo carousels.** Photo Mode is explicitly ineligible for Creator Rewards. No primary page found that says photo posts are ineligible for For You, or that they are boosted. A 35-image cap is repeated by tool blogs and was not on the camera-tools extract captured here. — [Creator Academy Rewards](https://www.tiktok.com/creator-academy/article/creator-rewards-program)
- **Series.** Creator Academy lists Series under user-pay monetization, next to Subscription. No page found that connects Series to For You ranking or to a distribution penalty. — [Creator Academy index, Series nav item](https://www.tiktok.com/creator-academy/article/guidelines-recommendation-system-intro)
- **US algorithm governance, 22–23 Jan 2026.** TikTok USDS Joint Venture LLC closed. ByteDance keeps 19.9%; Oracle, Silver Lake, and MGX hold 15% each. TikTok’s announcement, quoted by Reuters, The Verge, and the 23 Jan 2026 press text: the venture will “retrain, test, and update the content recommendation algorithm on U.S. user data,” secured in Oracle’s US cloud, and it has trust-and-safety authority for the US. This is a US-only training constraint. The public signal list was not rewritten in the pages read. POLITICO Magazine, 9 Oct 2026, reports continued interoperability so US creators can still be discovered globally, and that staff still work with the global organization; it also says it is unclear how much of the algorithm ByteDance still maintains. Forrester’s same-week note that the feed “will feel distinctly American” is an analyst prediction, not a measurement. US Creator Rewards terms dated in the index 20 July 2026 name TikTok USDS Joint Venture as the party that decides rewards criteria. — [Reuters, 23 Jan 2026](https://www.reuters.com/world/china/tiktok-reaches-deal-new-us-joint-venture-avoid-american-ban-2026-01-23/); [The Verge, 23 Jan 2026](https://www.theverge.com/tech/866437/tiktok-usds-bytedance-sale-oracle-mgx); [POLITICO Magazine, 9 Oct 2026](https://www.politico.com/news/magazine/2026/10/09/tiktok-trump-deal-us-china-01093142); [US Creator Rewards terms](https://www.tiktok.com/legal/page/global/creator-rewards-program-us/en)
- **Spark Ads / Promote, only where it changes organic reading.** Qualified Rewards views exclude paid views, promoted views, and artificial views. Promote does not count as the views that pay. A 2023 “2 million videos in Indonesia and Pakistan, disclosure label did not change performance” line appeared in a search snippet whose URL was an unrelated Adjust help article, so that study is **not** treated as verified here. Improper commercial disclosure can still be an eligibility issue under the integrity rules, separate from Spark delivery. — [Support, how rewards work](https://support.tiktok.com/en/business-and-creator/creator-rewards-program/how-rewards-work)
- **Historical engineering, not the 2026 consumer spec.** ByteDance’s Monolith paper (2022) describes a real-time recommendation stack (online training, collisionless embeddings). The Washington Post (10 Dec 2025) cites a 2022 ByteDance paper only as evidence that the company uses embedding-style recommenders. It is not a description of today’s For You weights. — [Washington Post, 10 Dec 2025](https://www.washingtonpost.com/technology/interactive/2025/tiktok-algorithm-video-map-interests/)

### Inferences
- For an AI video in 2026, the official risk order is: (1) For You ineligibility if the video is reused or only minimally transformed, (2) removal or restriction if realistic AI is unlabeled or misleading, (3) less exposure to viewers who turn the AI topic slider down, (4) no stated penalty for the label itself. “The algorithm hates AI” is not what TikTok says. “The algorithm hates unlabeled realistic AI and unoriginal reposts” is.
- Longer upload limits and the Rewards 1-minute rule pull monetized creators toward longer videos. For You still pays for finishing and time spent. A long video that people drop immediately is, on the 2020 sentence, a weak signal. A short video people finish can still be a strong one.
- US versus rest-of-world is now a real split in **who trains** the model, not in the published feature list. Do not assume a US test and a non-US test will match after 22 Jan 2026, and do not assume the public explanation changed.

### Gaps
- No TikTok A/B on labeled versus unlabeled AI reach. The support sentence is a policy assertion, not a published experiment. The auto label has no equivalent “won’t affect distribution” sentence on the consumer support page; Shop pages come closer.
- Whether 60-minute upload is universal or still rolling out by account was not settled by a single English help page. The Polish camera-tools page states 60 minutes as the upload cap without an eligibility caveat.
- Photo-carousel ranking versus video ranking: no official comparison found.
- Series and Subscription: no evidence found on organic distribution.
- Measured before/after of the US retraining on reach, completion, or content mix: not found. POLITICO’s interoperability quote means global discovery was an announced goal; it is not a metric.

## What should a creator watch in TikTok Studio in the first hour, and which metrics map to the signals?

### Takeaway
Map retention metrics to the signals TikTok says are heavy, map For You traffic share to whether distribution is actually happening, and treat likes as secondary. TikTok has not said the decision locks at 60 minutes. Ineligibility, if shown, overrides every engagement metric. Qualified-view and RPM figures are payout math, not the ranker.

### Cited Findings
- **Official mapping from product metrics to stated signals.**
  - Average watch time and total play time → “spend a certain amount of time viewing,” the interaction TikTok says is generally heavy. Creator Academy: watch time is weighted more than likes, comments, and shares; watch retention rate first. — [Creator Academy analytics article](https://www.tiktok.com/creator-academy/article/analytics-tool-video-performance); [Transparency appendix](https://www.tiktok.com/transparency/en/recommendation-system)
  - Watched-full-video percentage → the “finish” prediction, and the 2020 “finishes a longer video” strong indicator. Compare it to length. A high completion on a very short video is not automatically the strong case TikTok described. — [Newsroom, 18 June 2020](https://newsroom.tiktok.com/en-us/how-tiktok-recommends-videos-for-you)
  - Average watch time above the video’s duration → replays, which add time spent. Not a separately named weight. — inference from [Transparency appendix](https://www.tiktok.com/transparency/en/recommendation-system)
  - Likes, comments, shares → predicted like, comment, share. Academy: second to retention. — [Creator Academy analytics article](https://www.tiktok.com/creator-academy/article/analytics-tool-video-performance)
  - Favorites / saves, if shown → “favorite” prediction. — [Transparency appendix](https://www.tiktok.com/transparency/en/recommendation-system)
  - Profile visits and new follows from the video → “follow the author or interact with their profile.” — [Transparency appendix](https://www.tiktok.com/transparency/en/recommendation-system)
  - Traffic source For You vs Following vs profile vs Search vs sound → whether the recommendation pool is expanding (For You), whether only existing fans are watching (Following, profile), whether the sound is recruiting (sound), or whether queries are recruiting (Search). Academy tells creators to read this split and to reuse queries that already sent search traffic. — [Creator Academy analytics article](https://www.tiktok.com/creator-academy/article/analytics-tool-video-performance)
  - Ineligibility notice in analytics → the video is outside the candidate pool, so retention math will not produce a push. — [FYF standards, effective 24 Sep 2026](https://www.tiktok.com/community-guidelines/en/fyf-standards)
- **Rewards dashboard is a second screen.** Qualified For You views (unique, over 5 seconds, not “not interested,” not paid, not promoted, not fraudulent), RPM, search value, originality. A video can be widely recommended and still earn nothing if it is under one minute, Photo Mode, a Duet or Stitch, sponsored, or under 1,000 qualified views. Program countries named in the academy FAQ: United States, Mexico, Brazil, South Korea, Japan, Germany, United Kingdom, France. Account gates from the 2024 launch post, still repeated on support pages: 18 or older, 10,000 followers, 100,000 views in 30 days, personal account in good standing. — [Support, how rewards work](https://support.tiktok.com/en/business-and-creator/creator-rewards-program/how-rewards-work); [Newsroom, 18 Mar 2024](https://newsroom.tiktok.com/introducing-the-new-creator-rewards-program); [Creator Academy Rewards](https://www.tiktok.com/creator-academy/article/creator-rewards-program)
- **First hour: what is and is not official.** TikTok has not published a first-hour cutoff. It has said new or in-review uploads may be ineligible for a time, and that recency is a reason a video is shown. The viral.app panel (March–August 2026 posts) is the only large timing measurement found: zero views inside 30 minutes often recovered; zero views at 18–30 hours almost never reached 1,000. Practitioner guides that say “check likes in the first hour” are prioritizing a signal TikTok’s academy article ranks below retention. — [viral.app zero-view study](https://viral.app/blog/guides/tiktok-0-views-after-an-hour); [Newsroom, 18 June 2020](https://newsroom.tiktok.com/en-us/how-tiktok-recommends-videos-for-you)
- **Analytics latency is not an official number here.** A marketing blog says average watch time and traffic source can take 24–48 hours while likes move sooner. That delay was not confirmed on a TikTok page. If it is true, the first hour may only show views and raw interactions, with the metrics that matter arriving later. — [Graphed, 21 Dec 2025](https://www.graphed.com/blog/how-long-does-tiktok-analytics-take), unverified against TikTok
- **Public view counters are not the Studio truth.** Third-party APIs do not expose average watch time or completion; those sit in Studio or in authorized creator APIs. Public like counts therefore cannot reconstruct the leading signal. — [TikLiveAPI, 29 May 2026](https://www.tikliveapi.com/blog/tiktok-watch-time-completion-rate-public-api/)

### Inferences
- In the first session a creator can actually see: views (is distribution happening at all), likes/comments/shares (secondary interactions), and, as soon as Studio shows them, average watch time, watched-full-video, and the For You percentage. A rising view count with a collapsing average watch time is the pattern the official strong-indicator sentence predicts will stall. A flat view count with an ineligibility flag is a policy outcome, not a hook problem.
- Do not use Rewards qualified views as a proxy for For You rank. They deliberately drop sub-5-second views, repeat views from the same account, not-interested views, and all paid views. The organic ranker is asked to predict those behaviors, not to ignore them.
- Posting-time optimization is at most a way to have real viewers available while the video is still “created recently.” It is not, on the documents, a score feature.

### Gaps
- Exact Studio field names drift by app version (Average Watch Time vs average percentage watched vs retention curve). This note did not screenshot a 13 Oct 2026 Studio build. The metric families above are what Creator Academy and consistent third-party descriptions of the analytics screen agree on.
- Whether creators see a numeric Not Interested or report count: not found. The ineligibility state is the official negative flag that is documented.
- How soon after posting the ineligibility flag appears: not stated.
- No official “good” benchmark for average watch time or completion. Any percentage target in a growth blog (50%, 60%, 70%) is unsourced relative to TikTok.
