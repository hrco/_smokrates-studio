# Smokrates: seven-day TikTok, as of 14 October 2026

Scope: one new Personal account for a fictional instrumental artist (psychedelic chill dub, original audio, stylized poster character, no real person's face). The human publishes. Nothing here is a viral guarantee. Geography checked as Slovenia / EEA, with the US called out where the rule differs.

## Can Creator Rewards pay this account inside 7 days?

### Takeaway
No. The live Creator Academy eligibility article (published 18 August 2026, still the text returned on 14 October 2026) requires 10,000 followers, 100,000 video views in the last 30 days, a Personal account, age 18 or older, and residence in one of eight countries. Slovenia is not on that list. A video only earns if it is original, over one minute, and clears 1,000 qualifying views. Even an account that already qualified is not paid on day 7: the EEA terms calculate rewards in the following month.

### Cited Findings
- Eligibility article, `publishedAt` 2026-08-18: the program "is currently open to creators in the United States, the United Kingdom, Germany, Japan, South Korea, France, Mexico, and Brazil," who must be based there with an account registered there. Only Personal accounts qualify. Business accounts, organization accounts, and government or political accounts do not. Age is at least 18. The account needs at least 10,000 followers and 100,000 video views in the last 30 days, good standing, and original content. Unoriginal means account info or video "copied from others or has minimal original input or edits." Low quality explicitly includes split screens, meaningless reactions, low-quality images, and slide videos. A profile that contains personal or business contact information is an ineligible "Advertising" factor. Switching a Business account to Personal, then waiting, is required before applying: "you'll be able to apply for the Creator Rewards Program after 30 days." The account must be public. — [Creator Academy, eligibility](https://www.tiktok.com/creator-academy/article/eligibility)
- The same eight countries are repeated as the places a qualified view must come from, on the Creator Rewards article whose adjacent `publishedAt` is 2026-09-10. Qualified views need a watch of at least five seconds, exclude an "I'm not interested" tap, count multiple views from the same account as one, and count search views only at 30 seconds or more. Ads, paid promotions, and sponsored content do not qualify. A withdrawal request is "typically" processed within 72 hours, with no guarantee, and a bank can hold it longer. — [Creator Academy, Creator Rewards Program](https://www.tiktok.com/creator-academy/en/article/creator-rewards-program)
- A separate Academy article states the per-video bar: original high-quality content over 1 minute; at least 1,000 views on the For You feed; not a Duet or Stitch; not Photo Mode; not an ad, paid promotion, or sponsored video. That article's slug still says "creativity-program," but the heading is Creator Rewards. — [Creator Academy, what videos are eligible](https://www.tiktok.com/creator-academy/en/article/monetization-creativity-program-video-eligible)
- EEA program terms, last update April 2025: available "only in selected countries"; residence decides, and a VPN does not qualify. Join bars match the Academy (10,000 authentic followers, 100,000 authentic views in the prior 30 days, not a Business account). A rewarded video must be one minute or longer, reach at least 1,000 qualified views, not be a Duet, Stitch, Photo Mode, or sponsored post, and be "original content and produced entirely by the creator and/or adds new ideas." Qualified views are five seconds or more, not marked not interested, and unique per account. Rewards "will be calculated in the month following the date that the Rewards were collected" and "paid on the 15th of each month" if the payable amount is at least $50 USD or the local equivalent. — [EEA Creator Rewards terms](https://www.tiktok.com/legal/page/global/tiktok-creator-rewards-program-eea/en)
- The US legal-terms URL returned a 404 on 14 October 2026, so the US settlement calendar was not read from a US contract. The US is one of the eight countries on the Academy page. — [US terms URL, 404](https://www.tiktok.com/legal/page/global/tiktok-creator-rewards-program-us/en)
- Newsroom, 18 March 2024, still the live page: the Creativity Program Beta "is out of beta" and the launched product is the Creator Rewards Program. It pays "high-quality, original content that is over a minute long" on originality, play duration (watch time and finish rate), search value, and audience engagement, plus the audience's ad-watch value. Play duration "rewards accounts with content that is clear, and engaging, rather than favoring accounts with an excessive amount of videos." Join rules in that post: 18 or older, at least 10K followers, at least 100K views in the last 30 days, Personal account in good standing where the program is available. No country list in the post. — [TikTok Newsroom, 18 March 2024](https://newsroom.tiktok.com/introducing-the-new-creator-rewards-program)
- Neither the August 2026 eligibility article nor the video-requirements article names an AI label, or "significantly AI-edited," as a reason a video cannot earn. The ineligible-content list is unoriginal, low quality, contact-info advertising, clickbait, inappropriate, disturbing, manipulation, guideline violations, underage, Business, political. — [Creator Academy, eligibility](https://www.tiktok.com/creator-academy/article/eligibility); [Creator Academy, video eligibility](https://www.tiktok.com/creator-academy/en/article/monetization-creativity-program-video-eligible)

### Inferences
- For an individual in Slovenia, Creator Rewards cannot pay in 7 days or later, unless TikTok adds Slovenia. Germany and France are on the list. The US is on the list. Using a VPN to appear to live in one of the eight is forbidden by the EEA terms and would not make Slovenian viewers into qualified views, because the FAQ also limits qualified views to viewers in those eight countries.
- A brand-new account is also blocked by arithmetic that does not depend on country: it cannot already have 10,000 followers and 100,000 views "in the last 30 days" on day 7, because it has only seven days of history. The gate that makes a TikTok payout inside 7 days impossible is that pair of thresholds, plus, for this operator, the country list.
- The settlement calendar is a second lock even in an eligible country. EEA terms do not pay the month the views happen; they calculate the next month and pay on the 15th above $50. The Academy's 72-hour line is about a withdrawal that has already been requested, not about views becoming cash on day 7. The US contract page was a 404, so do not treat the 15th as verified US law; the 72-hour line still is not a day-7 deposit for an account that cannot enroll.
- Do not open the account as Business. The later rewards path wants Personal, and a switch starts a 30-day wait.
- Do not lengthen the dub videos to 60 seconds to chase this program. The account cannot enter it, and the rewarded length is a different product from a loop.
- AI-labeled video is not named as unpaid. What is named as unpaid is unoriginal or low-quality video, including a slide of low-quality images and a clip with "minimal original input." A repeated poster with a new caption and no new picture or new musical event is the risk, not the label itself.

### Gaps
- No Academy or Newsroom sentence was found that says an AI-labeled or significantly AI-edited video may earn, or may not earn. Absence from the ineligible list is not an affirmative clearance.
- The US Creator Rewards contract URL 404'd, so US tax forms, the $50 floor, and the 15th-of-the-month rule were not confirmed for a US account.
- No post after 18 August 2026 was found that adds Slovenia, the rest of the EEA, or any ninth country. The live page may lag an in-app test; the in-app eligibility screen is the check if the account is ever in another country.

## Which other TikTok programs can a day-7 account apply for?

### Takeaway
None of the follower-gated programs are a day-7 application for this account. Series requires an account at least 30 days old and 10,000 followers. The Subscription bonus requires over 10,000 followers and over 100,000 views in the last calendar month; the page does not publish a lower bar just to turn Subscription on. LIVE Gifts require age 18 (19 in South Korea), a country where Gifts exist, and eligibility to go LIVE. The August 2026 "Who can go LIVE?" article states age only, and it tells creators not to run a static or looping picture. TikTok Shop's Academy overview describes affiliate links and prints no follower number. The Creativity Program Beta is not a second program; the March 2024 Newsroom post says it left beta as Creator Rewards.

### Cited Findings
- Series, Academy article last updated 18 August 2026: at least 10,000 followers; 18 or older; account at least 30 days old; public Personal account, with Business accounts eligible "in select regions"; at least three public posts in the last 30 days; at least 1,000 views in the last 30 days; good standing; "Provide a link to any premium content you've sold. Not all creators will be required to provide this information." A Series holds up to 80 videos, each up to 20 minutes, with an optional free intro, after a safety and music review. The article does not contain an under-10,000 path. — [Creator Academy, Series](https://www.tiktok.com/creator-academy/en/article/monetization-series-overview)
- A web-search snippet of the Support Series URL said creators under 10,000 followers can apply by linking premium content already sold elsewhere. That Support URL returned 404 when fetched on 14 October 2026. — [Support Series URL, 404](https://support.tiktok.com/en/business-and-creator/tiktok-series/about-tiktok-series); contradicted on the follower floor by the Academy article above.
- Subscription Academy article: after app-store fees the creator receives 70 percent, with a possible extra 20 percent "bringing the maximum profit-sharing to 90%." The bonus, not clearly the right to join, requires over 10k followers, over 100k video views in the last calendar month, guideline compliance, and either the first sub-only video that month or at least three valid sub-only videos. Default price $5.99, range $2.99 to $99.99 USD per month. Joining is "you'll be guided through the eligibility requirements" in TikTok Studio. The base join rule is not printed. — [Creator Academy, Subscription](https://www.tiktok.com/creator-academy/en/article/Subscription)
- LIVE Gifts Support FAQ: to turn Gifts on, live where LIVE Gifts is available, be 18 or older (19 in South Korea), and be eligible to go LIVE. Government, politician, political-party, and other public-interest accounts cannot collect Diamonds this way. No follower number on this page. — [TikTok Support, LIVE Gifts](https://www.tiktok.com/support/faq_detail?category=web_tiktok_live&id=7543897462415579704)
- Going LIVE, Academy, last updated 18 August 2026: "you must be 18 years old or meet the age of majority in your country." No follower minimum in that answer. Gifts, once on, convert popularity into Diamonds and then "cash rewards" withdrawn to a bank or PayPal "based on your regions." Content rule: "avoiding prolonged inactivity, static or looping visuals, or content that isn't actively directed by you." Repeated failure, or failing verification, can end the LIVE. — [Creator Academy, Going LIVE](https://www.tiktok.com/creator-academy/en/article/Going-LIVE)
- TikTok Shop overview, last updated 18 August 2026: affiliate rewards from products linked in videos, LIVE, and a product showcase, "without changing your format," and "you don't need your own inventory." No follower, age, or country threshold in the article. A guessed US Shop Academy URL 404'd. — [Creator Academy, TikTok Shop](https://www.tiktok.com/creator-academy/en/article/monetization-shop-overview)
- Creativity Program: the only official status found is the 18 March 2024 Newsroom post that it is out of beta and replaced by Creator Rewards. The October 2026 Academy catalog still uses "creativity-program" in FAQ slugs for Creator Rewards. No separate Creativity Program application was found. — [TikTok Newsroom, 18 March 2024](https://newsroom.tiktok.com/introducing-the-new-creator-rewards-program)

### Inferences
- Day 7 cannot apply for Series, because the account is 7 days old, not 30, and will not have 10,000 followers by any honest reading of week-one data below. The 30-day account age is its own lock, separate from followers.
- Day 7 should not plan on Subscription income. The only numbered gate on the page is the bonus, and it is the same 10k / 100k neighborhood as Creator Rewards. Whether the Join button even appears in Slovenia is unpublished.
- A day-7 LIVE of the poster on a loop is the format the LIVE article tells creators not to run. Gifts also require the feature to be available where the operator lives. Slovenia was not on any Gifts country list retrieved. Do not treat LIVE as the 7-day paycheck.
- Shop affiliate is a product-link product. This account sells a piece of music, not someone else's inventory. The missing follower number is not a reason to bolt products onto the dub.
- Nothing here requires Promote, a follower purchase, or an engagement pod. Those are out.

### Gaps
- No official follower minimum for going LIVE was on the August 2026 Academy page. Blog posts still say 1,000 followers. That number is not used here because it was not on Help Center, Academy, or Newsroom text retrieved today.
- LIVE Gifts country list, Diamond-to-cash minimum, and how fast a first withdrawal arrives were not on the Gifts FAQ.
- Subscription's unpublished in-app join rule, and Shop's unpublished eligibility rule, could be lower than 10,000 in some countries. They were not found, so a day-7 application is not claimed.

## Does the original sound, SoundOn, or a distributor pay, and does TikTok clearance cover YouTube?

### Takeaway
Posting the dub as the video's own sound is allowed when the artist owns it. No official page retrieved says that a new account earns money when other people use that original sound. The profile Music tab is a later claim, not a day-7 switch: the artist must already be in TikTok's music library, prove it with a distributor or streaming screenshot, and wait up to 30 days for an answer. The Commercial Music Library, updated July 2025, is a separate pre-cleared catalog for businesses, and it is a TikTok license, not a YouTube license. The live UMG alliance page promises pay for UMG artists and songwriters and does not mention AI music or voice clones. SoundOn and the 2026 DistroKid or TuneCore help pages did not load.

### Cited Findings
- Academy sound article, last updated 18 August 2026: use "licensed music or original audio that you have the rights to." It points at the Commercial Music Library, "over 1 million sounds and songs." It does not say an original sound pays the uploader. — [Creator Academy, the importance of sound](https://www.tiktok.com/creator-academy/en/article/the-importance-of-sound)
- Commercial Music Library, last updated July 2025: about 1 million songs, "free for businesses to use," sourced from independents, emerging artists, and music houses. "Businesses cannot use the general music library for commercial usage." Commercial TikTok activities that must use the CML, or another license, are organic business posts (including duet, react, or stitch), video ads, and branded content. Using "original sound from other users or other licensed sounds" in organic or paid business content needs a separate license. The article never mentions YouTube, and it never says the artist who first posted a sound is paid when a viewer reuses it. — [TikTok Ads Help, Commercial Music Library](https://ads.tiktok.com/help/article/commercial-music-library)
- Music tab, Academy article last updated 18 August 2026: an artist uses it "to promote your music and grow your listener base." Getting the tab requires an artist-account application in TikTok Studio, then Artist Hub. The form searches "the artist profile in the TikTok library," the artist taps Claim, then submits a screenshot from a streaming platform or music distributor that shows the artist name and album or track titles, to prove ownership. "You will receive feedback within 30 days." After approval, "your music list will automatically sync." The article gives no royalty rate for plays of that tab, and no way to create the library entry from a video upload alone. — [Creator Academy, music tab](https://www.tiktok.com/creator-academy/en/article/music-tab-introduction)
- UMG page fetched 14 October 2026, title "TikTok and Universal Music Group announce expanded global alliance": equitable compensation for UMG recording artists and Universal Music Publishing Group songwriters, and the right for users to use clips from UMG's catalog. The HTML has no article date (footer copyright says 2025) and no occurrence of AI, voice, or clone. — [TikTok Newsroom, UMG alliance](https://newsroom.tiktok.com/en-us/tiktok-and-universal-music-group-announce-expanded-global-alliance)
- AI audio rule, Creator Academy label article: disclosure is required when "AI-generated audio mimics the voice of a real person." Disclosure is not needed for "artistic styles, like anime," small corrections, or generic text-to-speech that is not a recognizable real person's voice. Likeness of a private individual without consent is not allowed. — [Creator Academy, AI-generated content label](https://www.tiktok.com/creator-academy/en/article/ai-generated-content-label)
- `tiktok.com/soundon` returned a captcha shell. The guessed SoundOn Newsroom URL returned a 500. TuneCore's TikTok article URL 404'd. DistroKid's TikTok URLs returned a tracker pixel, not a help article. — [SoundOn](https://www.tiktok.com/soundon); [SoundOn newsroom attempt](https://newsroom.tiktok.com/sound-on-a-new-platform-for-music-artists)

### Inferences
- The dub should be the sound on every post, because it is the artist's recording and the artist has the rights. That is a rights fact, not a paycheck. The Music tab cannot be the week-one plan: feedback takes up to 30 days, and the claim form only finds an artist who is already in TikTok's library, which means a distributor or streaming profile already exists to screenshot. Starting that distributor delivery is the off-platform setup that does not depend on TikTok followers. It was not shown to pay inside 7 days.
- Until a library entry exists, the original sound on the videos is the only official way the track is on the account. Do not wait for the Music tab to post.
- A business account that wanted to use someone else's TikTok sound in an ad would need the CML or a direct license. This project is the opposite case: an individual posting their own track on a Personal account. The CML is not how Smokrates gets paid.
- Do not clone a real singer's voice. A stylized poster is in the "artistic styles, like anime" bucket only while it does not look like a real person. A photoreal face or a real place is a label, and a private person's likeness is a ban.
- "Cleared for TikTok, therefore cleared for YouTube" is not supported. The only license text retrieved is explicitly about commercial use on TikTok. It does not grant YouTube. The distributor sentence that Content ID is a separate opt-in was not re-read from DistroKid or TuneCore today, so it is not stated as a 2026 fact.

### Gaps
- No 2026 SoundOn, DistroKid, or TuneCore page was retrieved, so this note cannot state the current royalty path, the TikTok sound-match rate, or a dated confirmation that a TikTok delivery does not include YouTube Content ID.
- No later UMG/TikTok AI-music or voice-clone agreement was found. The alliance page may be years old; it has no visible date. Do not treat it as a 2026 AI policy.
- Whether TikTok pays an independent, non-UMG artist anything when strangers reuse an original sound remains unanswered by the pages that loaded.

## What growth tactics are actually measured for a new music or faceless account?

### Takeaway
Two large panels, not TikTok's ranker, are the evidence. Buffer's October 2025 frequency study (11.4 million posts, more than 150,000 accounts) finds the median post stays near 500 views no matter how often the account posts; extra posts raise the chance of one lucky post, and the efficient step is from once a week to 2–5 a week. viral.app's panel of 3.3 million public videos (October 2025–August 2026, about 81,000 tracked accounts) finds original sound is now most TikToks and does not lose views to licensed music inside the same account. Neither panel is a random sample of brand-new music accounts. Both are vendor panels.

### Cited Findings
- Buffer, page titled for 2026 but describing the 2025 study by Julian Winternheimer: 11.4 million TikTok posts, more than 150,000 accounts, fixed-effects regression that compares an account to itself. Versus once a week: 2–5 posts a week, 17% more views per post; 6–10, 29%; 11 or more, 34%. Median views per post: 489, 506, 487, and 459. 90th percentile: 3,722, 6,983, 10,092, and 14,401. Winternheimer: "Posting more helps — but mostly because it increases your chances of getting lucky. TikTok is heavy-tailed." The article says the steepest return for the work is 1 post to 2–5, that quality matters more than quantity, and that the relative gains held after controlling for account size. It also says the p90 figures are not what a small account is used to seeing, and that "even new creators can have viral posts." The panel is Buffer's own data, not all of TikTok. — [Buffer, how often to post on TikTok](https://buffer.com/resources/how-often-should-you-post-on-tiktok/)
- viral.app, published time on the page 13 June 2025, body covering October 2025–August 2026: 3.3 million public videos with at least the tracking their customers run, about 81,000 accounts. This is a vendor panel. Median TikTok length across all tracked videos is 16 seconds. Share of TikToks: under 10 seconds 26.9%, 10–29 seconds 42.1%, 30–59 seconds 20.3%, 1–3 minutes 10.1%. Share that reached 10 times that account's own median: under 10 seconds 4.5%, 10–29 seconds 4.7%, 30–59 seconds 5.7%, 1–3 minutes 7.5%. A 1–3 minute TikTok hit 1 million views 4 times as often as one under 10 seconds (1.85% vs 0.43%), but inside the same account it got 1.06 times the views of that account's videos under a minute, and it was better in 57% of about 4,300 accounts. Original sound rose from 65.4% to 70.1% of TikTok videos "with no difference in views." Inside the same account, licensed music got 1.01 times the median views of original sound (about 11,000 accounts). Sounds used by 100 or more tracked accounts broke out to 10 times the account median 5.3% of the time, versus 4.9% for sounds no other tracked account used. Months with twice the posts: 0.94 times the views per post, and 2.4 times the total views. — [viral.app, short-form video trends](https://viral.app/blog/insights/short-form-video-trends)
- Newsroom rewards formula, 18 March 2024, is about pay, not the For You ranker: it says the formula does not favor "an excessive amount of videos." Search value is one of the four pay metrics, and the post points creators at Creator Search Insights for phrases people search. That tool pays only if the account is already in the rewards program. — [TikTok Newsroom, 18 March 2024](https://newsroom.tiktok.com/introducing-the-new-creator-rewards-program); [TikTok Newsroom, Creator Search Insights](https://newsroom.tiktok.com/creator-search-insights)
- Academy AI label article, and the Support FAQ: turning on the creator AI label "won't affect the distribution of your video" if the video does not violate the Community Guidelines and, on the Academy page, stays For You-eligible. That is TikTok's policy sentence, not a measured experiment. An auto label from Content Credentials cannot be removed. — [Creator Academy, AI label](https://www.tiktok.com/creator-academy/en/article/ai-generated-content-label); [TikTok Support, AI-generated content](https://www.tiktok.com/support/faq_detail?id=7636670084747893268)

### Inferences
- For this account, 3 to 5 strong posts in the week match the only efficient step Buffer measured. Seven distinct posts (one a day) sit in the 6–10 band, where the median was still about 487 and the average rose because a few posts did better. Twenty-one posts (three a day for seven days) sit in 11+, where the median was 459, slightly worse, and the higher ceiling assumes the extra posts are still worth finishing. Do not fill the day with a second and third weak export. The studio may render up to three. Publish one, unless the second is a different idea.
- Original sound is not a view penalty in the viral.app panel. A trending sound would replace the only thing this account is selling. Use the dub as the sound on every post.
- Do not stretch a loop to 60 seconds. Inside an account, going from under a minute to 1–3 minutes was a 6 percent view difference in that panel, and Creator Rewards cannot be entered. Under 10 seconds is the right band only when the picture and the bar actually loop. One claim and one payoff stays in the 15–30 second band the studio already uses, which sits inside the 10–29 second slice that is 42% of TikToks in the panel.
- A series helps only as a recognizable character with a new event. The rewards page's "minimal original input" and "slide videos" lines are the risk if every day is the same poster and a new caption.
- Search phrases belong in the caption as the name of the music, not as a medical promise. Creator Search Insights is for enrolled rewards accounts and for topic research. It is not a reason to write "sleep" or "focus" as a health claim.

### Gaps
- No study retrieved was limited to new music accounts, faceless accounts, or week-one accounts. Applying Buffer's medians to Smokrates on day 1 is an extrapolation.
- No primary source was found for a first-frame hook percentage, a best hour, or a hashtag count that this note is willing to repeat. Those claims were dropped.
- TikTok has not published a completion-rate table by length in the pages fetched here. viral.app says what was posted and what views it got, not watch time.

## What does week 1 actually look like, and is "viral and paid" blocked?

### Takeaway
"Viral and paid by TikTok in 7 days" is structurally blocked. The pay block is the Creator Rewards gate: 10,000 followers and 100,000 views in 30 days, in a country Slovenia is not, on videos over a minute, with EEA money calculated the following month. Virality itself is not promised and not disproved: Buffer says a new creator can still have a viral post, and the median post in their mixed panel is about 500 views, not 10,000 followers. No sourced median of followers gained in the first 7 days of a new account was found.

### Cited Findings
- Join thresholds and the eight-country list, including the absence of Slovenia, are the Academy eligibility article of 18 August 2026, quoted above. — [Creator Academy, eligibility](https://www.tiktok.com/creator-academy/article/eligibility)
- EEA payment timing: calculated the month after the views, paid on the 15th if at least $50. — [EEA Creator Rewards terms, April 2025](https://www.tiktok.com/legal/page/global/tiktok-creator-rewards-program-eea/en)
- Buffer medians of 489 to 506 views per post, and the statement that new creators can still have a viral post, are the frequency study above. The author flags that the 90th-percentile view counts are not the experience of a small account. Those medians are not a new-account forecast. — [Buffer](https://buffer.com/resources/how-often-should-you-post-on-tiktok/)
- viral.app's "median views at day 7: TikTok down 3%" is the age of a video in a tracked-account panel, not the first week of a new account. — [viral.app](https://viral.app/blog/insights/short-form-video-trends)
- Series cannot be applied for before the account is 30 days old. — [Creator Academy, Series](https://www.tiktok.com/creator-academy/en/article/monetization-series-overview)

### Inferences
- Separate three clocks. Inside 7 days, TikTok's own programs do not pay this account: Rewards and the Subscription bonus need 10,000 followers and on the order of 100,000 views, Series needs a 30-day-old account as well as 10,000 followers, and a looping LIVE is against the LIVE content rule. In 7 days the human can start a distributor delivery so the track can enter TikTok's music library and streaming stores later. The Music tab application answers within 30 days and only works if that library entry already exists, so the tab is not a day-7 feature. Store payouts were not shown to land inside 7 days, and no 2026 DistroKid, TuneCore, or SoundOn rate card was retrieved. A direct sale on a shop the human already runs also sits outside TikTok's follower gates; no store help page was fetched, so it is not a priced plan.
- A viral post is a tail event Buffer describes, not a plan. Planning as if day 7 ends at 10,000 followers contradicts the only medians in hand, which are about 500 views on a typical post in a mixed, not brand-new, panel.
- If the operator were in the US, Germany, France, or another listed country, the country lock would lift and the follower, view, length, and settlement locks would remain. Seven days still cannot produce a Creator Rewards balance.

### Gaps
- No median or percentile of followers, or of views, for accounts in their first 7 days was found in Buffer, viral.app, TikTok Newsroom, or Help Center. Do not invent one.
- No RPM figure for music or faceless video was taken from TikTok. The Academy describes a dashboard RPM for people already in the program. It is irrelevant until the gates are met.

## What should days 1–7 and the profile contain?

### Takeaway
One job a day, the original dub as the sound, 15–30 seconds unless the day is a true loop under 10 seconds. Skip a day rather than post a weak extra. The profile is a public Personal account named Smokrates, with a one-line bio and no contact pitch. Label AI only when a frame looks like a real person or a real place; an anime-like poster does not, unless TikTok's automatic label attaches and cannot be removed.

### Cited Findings
- Length and original-sound measurements are the viral.app panel cited above. Frequency and the flat median are the Buffer study cited above. The over-one-minute rewards rule is the Academy video-eligibility article, and this account cannot enter that program.
- Realistic AI must be labeled. Community Guidelines: creators must label "AI-generated or significantly edited content that shows realistic-looking scenes or people." Unlabeled content may be removed, restricted, or labeled. — [Community Guidelines, Integrity and Authenticity](https://www.tiktok.com/community-guidelines/en/integrity-authenticity)
- Academy label article: disclosure includes a real person's mimicked voice, and does not include "artistic styles, like anime" or generic TTS that is not a known person. The creator toggle "won't affect" distribution if the post stays within the rules. The label cannot be edited off after posting. — [Creator Academy, AI label](https://www.tiktok.com/creator-academy/en/article/ai-generated-content-label)
- Support FAQ: creators are encouraged to label content completely generated or significantly edited by AI. It then points at the Community Guidelines, which require a label for realistic AIGC.
